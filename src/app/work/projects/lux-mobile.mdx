## Overview

LUX Mobile is a modern e-commerce website concept created for a gadget and electronics store.

The goal was to create a clean and easy-to-use shopping experience where customers can explore products, browse categories, view prices, and contact the store directly through WhatsApp.

## What I built

- Modern responsive storefront
- Product categories
- Featured products
- Product pricing
- Mobile-friendly layout
- Clear product presentation
- WhatsApp enquiry flow
- Responsive design for mobile and desktop

## Design approach

I focused on creating a clean and modern interface that makes it easy for visitors to discover products and quickly find what they need.

The layout was designed to work smoothly across phones, tablets and desktop screens.

## My role

**Web Designer & Developer**

I designed and built the website with a focus on responsive layouts, visual hierarchy and a simple user experience.
